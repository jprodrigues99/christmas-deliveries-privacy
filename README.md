# Privacy Policy — Christmas Deliveries

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

Christmas Deliveries is a mobile game developed and published by JohnRodgsGamer. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the game.

## 1. Information Collection

Christmas Deliveries does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play.

Game settings, preferences, and high scores are stored locally on the user's device.

The game integrates Unity In-App Purchasing and Google Play Billing to provide optional in-app purchases. During purchase-related operations, these services may process information such as:

- Purchase history and transaction details.
- Device and application identifiers.
- Device model and operating system information.
- Language and regional settings.
- IP address and approximate location.
- Technical information necessary to operate purchase services.

## 2. Use of Information

Information may be processed to:

- Provide and maintain purchase functionality.
- Process and verify transactions.
- Prevent fraud and duplicate transactions.
- Provide technical support.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. In-App Purchases

Christmas Deliveries offers optional purchases that allow players to support the developer.

Payments are processed through Google Play. The developer does not directly collect or store credit card numbers or other payment credentials.

## 4. Advertising and Analytics

Christmas Deliveries does not display advertisements or use dedicated gameplay analytics services.

The developer does not sell personal information or use it for personalized advertising.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The game uses services provided by Unity Technologies and Google Play. These providers may process information necessary to operate their services.

For more information, please review their privacy policies:

Unity:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Google:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game preferences and high scores remain on the device until they are reset, the application's data is cleared, or the game is uninstalled.

Purchase-related information may be retained by Unity and Google as necessary to provide their services, prevent fraud, maintain transaction records, and comply with legal obligations.

Users may contact the developer to request access to or deletion of applicable personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

## 7. Data Security

The developer takes reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. Purchase processing is handled through established third-party payment services.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Christmas Deliveries does not require account registration or directly request personal information from children.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of in-app purchases.

If you believe personal information relating to a child has been collected improperly, please contact the developer.

Depending on your country and applicable laws, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information.
